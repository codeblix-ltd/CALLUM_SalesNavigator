Help

How Callum Scout works

Follow these steps in order. Callum Scout saves your progress as it works.

Keep LinkedIn open and signed in before you start.

Before your first run

Set it up once

  1. 1
    Sign in

    Use the username and password your manager gave you.

  2. 2
    Choose your LinkedIn plan

    Choose Free or Premium. If you choose Premium, you can turn personal AI connection notes on or off. No Premium account check is needed.

  3. 3
    Choose comment review

    Turn it on if you want to check each comment before it is posted.

Every day

What happens after you click Start today’s work

  1. 1
    Check old sent requests

    It syncs your sent requests and withdraws matching requests that are over 30 days old.

  2. 2
    Check accepted connections

    It checks connections from the last 6 months only and stops there. It saves the LinkedIn email when one is available.

  3. 3
    Open one lead

    It opens the lead’s LinkedIn profile and checks the connection status. If you are already connected, it checks contact details instead.

  4. 4
    Like and comment on recent posts

    It checks only the latest 3 posts without scrolling farther. It verifies the post date twice and skips anything over 3 months old.

  5. 5
    Send the connection request

    It always returns to the profile and sends the request. If AI notes are on, it creates a short personal note from that lead’s profile. If the note or a comment is skipped, the connection request still continues.

  6. 6
    Save progress and move on

    It repeats the same order for the next lead until your daily limit is reached or there are no more leads.

After the run

You can check your work

Open the lead dashboard to see each lead and its current step. If you pause the run, Resume continues from the last safe step.

After the first lead finishes, the popup and purple automation window show an estimated finish time based on the average time per completed lead.

If a lead needs attention, use Retry failed leads. Callum Scout waits and checks the LinkedIn profile up to three times. If the lead still fails, open it in the lead dashboard to retry it alone or mark it rejected.

Later follow-ups, lead checks, and questions for the team stay in the extension for you to finish.

Close this tab when you are done.
