# Callum Scout support rules

These rules apply to every scout-facing bug-report reply:

- Address the person directly. Do not call the recipient "the scout" or describe
  them in the third person. Use "you" and the person's name only when helpful.
- Give the exact action to take and the expected result. Keep the reply short,
  plain, and operational.
- Do not mention servers, backends, gateways, databases, logs, deployments,
  parsers, code, or other implementation details unless the user explicitly asks
  to share them with the staff member.
- Do not explain an uncertain technical cause as fact. Put technical evidence,
  remaining unknowns, and verification details in the internal investigation
  note, not the scout-facing reply.
- Do not say the issue is fixed until the relevant workflow is verified. If only
  a workaround is available, state only the steps the person should take.
- Prefer one short paragraph of two to four sentences. Do not include release or
  reinstall instructions unless the person actually needs them.
