Callum Scout Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 August 2026

Callum Scout is an internal lead-workflow assistant for authorized sales scouts. It is used to work through assigned LinkedIn leads and record the resulting workflow state in the publisher's lead system.

Information handled

Depending on the features used, Callum Scout handles:

  • account information used to sign in, including a scout username and authentication session tokens;
  • assigned lead information such as names, profile URLs, titles, companies, and original or work email addresses;
  • visible LinkedIn website content needed for the workflow, including profile information, post text, post URLs, connection information, and contact-info results; and
  • workflow activity such as profile visits, likes, comments, connection requests, accepted or withdrawn requests, saved contact information, settings, and error or progress events.

How the information is used

  • to show the authorized scout's assigned lead queue and progress;
  • to open and operate the LinkedIn pages required for the assigned lead workflow;
  • to generate comment drafts from post text when the scout requests that feature; and
  • to save workflow results, enforce account-specific limits, recover an interrupted run, and provide support and security functions.

Sharing and service providers

Callum Scout sends the information needed for these functions over HTTPS to the publisher's authenticated Convex backend. When a comment draft is requested, the relevant post text is sent through the publisher's secured Codex gateway so a draft can be returned. These transfers are limited to operating and improving the extension's single purpose. We do not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use it for creditworthiness or lending decisions.

Local storage and security

The extension stores its current session tokens, workflow checkpoints, cached dashboard state, and user-selected settings in Chrome local storage. The sign-in password is sent for authentication and is not retained by the extension after the sign-in request. Network transfers use HTTPS, and access to the backend is authenticated and limited to the signed-in scout's authorized data.

Retention and deletion

Workflow records are retained in the publisher's lead system for the organization's operational recordkeeping. Signing out removes the local session from the extension. To request access, correction, or deletion of account or workflow data, use the publisher contact email listed on the Callum Scout Chrome Web Store item.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Chrome and LinkedIn pages adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide or improve the extension's disclosed lead-workflow features, and is not sold, used for unrelated purposes, used for personalized advertising, or used for creditworthiness or lending decisions.

Changes

We may update this policy when the extension's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with any material revision.
